ABOUT US

Background statement/history

Sinethemba Hope Organisation is a faith based organisation (FBO) which was founded by Pastor Ndumiso Ngema as a social wing of the Covenant Fellowship Church International. It was established in 2006 after the church realised that its members were either affected or infected by HIV and AIDS. In 2008 it was formally registered and decided to expand its services to the rest of the Pongola community. Sinethemba Hope Organization is based in Pongola. The organisation operates from the church premises in Pongola. It focuses on issues of domestic violence, drug abuse and gender based violence (GBV). Sinethemba has previously received sponsorship from the Department of Social Development (DSD) in the form of food parcels to be given to the poor, and also received a donation of office equipment from another organisation.

The activities of Sinethemba Hope Organization include providing information on HIV and AIDS to the community, churches, schools and families, sex workers and their clients, in work places, public and privately owned businesses. It also seeks to increase access to HIV and STI prevention services, and provide social and structural interventions to HIV, tuberculosis (TB) and sexually transmitted infections (STI) prevention Its current projects include among others, HIV/AIDS prevention, STIs and TB interventions. Apart from SHIPP, Sinethemba Hope Organization is also working with the National Department of Health (NDoH).

Since its formation, the communities in which Sinethemba Hope Organization operates have benefited from a lot of services which include intervention in HIV and AIDS through various methods which include dialogues, awareness and distribution of condoms.

The organisation operates from the church premises.

Organisational Vision and Mission

Vision

To be a leading organization that transforms and instils positive attitude, hope and determination in communities

Mission

We contribute in rebuilding the broken society, healing the spiritual, emotional, physical, intellectual and economic well-being of the communities.
