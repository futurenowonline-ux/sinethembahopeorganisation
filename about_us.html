Background & History

A Grassroots Vision Born Out of Faith and Compassion

Sinethemba Hope Organisation is a faith-based organisation (FBO) founded by Pastor Ndumiso Ngema as a dedicated social wing of the Covenant Fellowship Church International. Established in 2006, the initiative arose when church leadership recognized the profound impact of HIV/AIDS and poverty on families within the Pongola community.

Formally registered as an independent NPO in 2008, Sinethemba expanded its outreach to address domestic violence, youth substance abuse, and gender-based violence (GBV). Over the years, the organisation has collaborated with the Department of Social Development (DSD) and the National Department of Health (NDoH) to deliver health education, food parcel relief, school uniforms, and community sporting leagues.

Operating directly from church and community premises in Pongola, KwaZulu-Natal.

Our Vision

To be a leading grassroots organisation that transforms lives and instils positive attitude, hope, and determination in vulnerable communities across South Africa.

Our Mission

To contribute meaningfully to rebuilding broken societies by restoring the spiritual, emotional, physical, intellectual, and economic well-being of youth and families.

Operational Methodology

The “H-O-P-E” Project Framework

All programs at Sinethemba Hope Organisation are structured under our 4-pillar acronym:

H

Healing Services

Home-based care, sick identification, family counseling, bereavement support, and HIV/STI education.

Care • Support
O

Outreach & Uniforms

School uniform distributions, stationery packs, and community dialogues across rural schools.

Schools • Dignity
P

People Development

Income generation through communal food gardens, handcrafts, and vocational skill workshops.

Skills • Livelihood
E

Evangelism & Sports

Mobilizing youth through soccer, netball, music, poetry, and positive life-skills development.

Youth • Sport
