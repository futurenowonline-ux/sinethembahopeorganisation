PROJECT DETAILS

Since its formation, the communities in which Sinethemba Hope Organization operates have benefited from a lot of services which include intervention in HIV and AIDS through various methods which include dialogues, awareness and distribution of condoms. Sinethemba Hope Organization has also created and provided employment opportunities to the youths and other community members thereby alleviating poverty in their families.
